Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

TriggerFlow ("the App") is designed with privacy as its core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect any personal information, location history, or usage analytics. All operations are performed locally on your device.

2. Permissions Usage

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): This permission is required by the Android Operating System (Android 8.1+) to read the SSID (Name) of the currently connected WiFi network. TriggerFlow uses this strictly to determine if you are on a "Trusted" network. Your location is never recorded or shared.
  • WireGuard Control: Used solely to send "Up" or "Down" commands to the official WireGuard application installed on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

4. Contact

For questions regarding privacy, please open an issue on our GitHub Repository.

TriggerFlow is open source software.

Not affiliated with the WireGuard project.
